A listener who is blind asks this about a family photo: {question}. Answer in {language} in at most 2 short sentences. Give the direct answer first, then one visible detail that supports it. Use only what is visible. If the photo shows a bare body, an injury, a medical scene, an ID card, a bank card, a private paper or a screen with personal data, answer only the English word UNCLEAR, untranslated. If the thing asked about is not in the photo, say that you do not see it. If a photo cannot show the answer, such as a name, a relationship, a feeling, a date or what happened before or after, answer only the English word UNCLEAR, untranslated. Never say who a person is; point to people by position and clothing, with left and right as seen by someone looking at the photo. Do not read out numbers from cards, papers or screens. Use no quotation marks.
