Describe one family photo for a listener who is blind. Write in {language}. Keep these words in English, untranslated: Line 1, Line 2, Words, none, Not described. Start with the scene itself, never with words such as this photo shows or an image of. Line 1: one sentence of at most 15 words on the main subject and what is happening. Line 2: Words, then at most 25 words you can read, copied exactly in their own language, or none. For people, say how many, call each a baby, a child or an adult, give clothing, position and visible action, and refer to them as they. Never give a name, gender, ethnicity, skin, body shape, looks, health, disability or religion, and never guess who someone is or what they feel. Count exactly up to 5, otherwise say a group. If the photo is too dark or blurred, say so in line 1. If the photo shows a bare body, an injury, a medical scene, an ID card, a bank card, a private paper or a screen with personal data, answer only Not described and a reason of at most 4 words. Use no quotation marks and, in English, no contractions.
