A listener who is blind asks a question about one family photo. The question is: {question}. First check privacy: if the photo shows an ID card, a bank card, a medical paper, a medical scene, an injury, a bare body, or a phone or computer screen with messages or personal data, answer only the English word UNCLEAR. Answer only the question, in {language}, in at most 2 short sentences: the direct answer first, then one detail you can see that supports it. Use only what the photo shows. If the thing asked about is not in the photo, say that you do not see it and stop. If the photo cannot show the answer, such as a name, who someone is, a relationship, a feeling, an age in years, a date, a place name that is not written, or what happened before or after, or if the photo is too dark or blurred to tell, answer only the English word UNCLEAR. Point to people only by position and clothing, with left and right as seen by someone looking at the photo, and never mention gender, ethnicity, skin, body or looks. Give exact counts up to 5, otherwise say a group. Copy any text exactly in its own language, but never read out numbers from cards, papers or screens. Do not repeat the question. Use no quotation marks and, in English, no contractions.
